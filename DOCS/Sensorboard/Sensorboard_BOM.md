# ThermoSort Sensor Board

## Passive Components

### Capacitors

- 4x 100nF 0402 capacitors
- 1x 10uF 0402 capacitor

### Resistors

- 4x 10kΩ 0402 resistors
- 2x 40kΩ 0402 resistors

## Sensors

- 1x MLX90640ESF-BAA-000-TU thermal camera
- 2x CUSA-R80-120-2200-TH ultrasonic receivers
- 2x CUSA-T80-120-2200-TH ultrasonic transmitters

## Signal Conditioning

- 1x INA2332AIPWT instrumentation amplifier
- 1x TLV9062IDR operational amplifier
